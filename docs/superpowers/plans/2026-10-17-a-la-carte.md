# A La Carte Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task.

**Goal:** Order priced extras for a booked meal and settle their itemized booking bill.
**Architecture:** Owner catalogue and immutable order lines, scoped by mess; transactional, idempotent orders issue cumulative bills and preserve prior payments.
**Tech Stack:** FastAPI, SQLAlchemy/Decimal, React, existing Dialog, BillWorkflow and localization.
**Spec:** ../specs/2026-10-17-a-la-carte-design.md

## Global Constraints
Retain guest charges, base prices, activation, acceptance, headcounts, membership/address gates and existing payment review. No standalone extra checkout, refunds, new dependencies, deployment or commits. Preserve all unrelated edits.

## Review Focus
- Extra order retries cannot charge twice, even after item prices change.
- Archived items remain readable in old bills and order history.
- Pending proof and old paid bills cannot mark extras paid.
- Pause/skip/booking edits cannot erase confirmed extra commitments.
- Cross-mess IDs and inactive/single-meal-unaccepted bookings cannot order.
